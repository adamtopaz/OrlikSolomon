# Mathematical regression coverage

These are the nine examples required by the final regression paragraph of
[`blueprint/arrangement_cohomology_lean_blueprint.tex`](../blueprint/arrangement_cohomology_lean_blueprint.tex).
Every listed Lean module is imported by the library root, so `scripts/check.sh` builds it,
audits its declarations and runs the environment linters. `scripts/replay.sh` replays it
through the kernel. This inventory records proved statements; it adds no mathematical blueprint.

Names below are under `OrlikSolomon`. Cohomological statements quantify over a supplied
`OrdinaryTheory`; they do not construct a named nonzero cohomology realization. The actual
`Sanity.zeroTheory` instantiations test consistency and degenerate coefficients.

| Required example | Declarations and what they check |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Empty affine arrangement, including dimension zero | [`Examples/Degenerate`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/Degenerate.lean): `DegenerateExamples.emptyCohomologyEquiv` identifies the coefficient algebra with the empty complement's cohomology in every finite dimension. A literal zero-dimensional instance is included. |
| 2. One affine hyperplane | [`Examples/Exterior`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/Exterior.lean): `ExteriorExamples.singletonExteriorEquiv` gives the full exterior algebra on the unique label. `singlePointExteriorEquiv` instantiates it on the actual `PresentationExamples.singlePoint` fixture, with its generator formula. Concrete `ZMod 2` and `ZMod 4` square-zero checks use that singleton; `singlePoint_generator_sq_zmod_four` names the latter. A logarithmic square-zero check with `ZMod 4` coefficients is in `Examples/ProjectiveOrdinary`. |
| 3. Coordinate hyperplanes | [`Examples/Exterior`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/Exterior.lean): `ExteriorExamples.coordinateExteriorEquiv` gives the full exterior algebra on all coordinate labels and preserves generators. [`Examples/CoordinatePolynomials`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/CoordinatePolynomials.lean): `Examples.card_coordinateArrangement` counts those labels and `Examples.poincarePolynomial_coordinateArrangement` gives `(1 + X)^n`. Empty coordinate types include dimension zero. |
| 4. Two parallel affine lines | [`Examples/Parallel`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/Parallel.lean): `PresentationExamples.parallelLines_product`, `parallelLines_difference` and `poincarePolynomial_parallelLines` prove product zero, difference nonzero over every nonzero coefficient ring, and `1 + 2 X`. The characteristic-two difference is instantiated explicitly. |
| 5. Three concurrent lines | [`Examples/AffineLines`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/AffineLines.lean): `AffineLineExamples.threeConcurrent_boundary` proves the alternating relation over any coefficient ring, and `poincarePolynomial_threeConcurrent` gives `1 + 3 X + 2 X²`. The fixture is `PresentationExamples.threeConcurrent`; [`Examples/Cokernel`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/Cokernel.lean) proves its distinct old labels have equal nonempty traces and that replacement is independent of the old label. |
| 6. Three nonconcurrent lines, no parallel pair | [`Examples/AffineLines`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/AffineLines.lean): `mem_triangle` identifies the three lines by public equations; `triangle_inter_triple`, `triangle_triple` and `poincarePolynomial_triangle` prove empty triple intersection, zero triple product and `1 + 3 X + 3 X²`. `triangle_boundary_ne` detects the absence of a degree-two boundary relation over every nonzero coefficient ring, including characteristic two. `triangle_unguarded` shows why omitting the nonempty-intersection guard would introduce that wrong relation. |
| 7. Two points over F₂ | [`Examples/Sanity`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/Sanity.lean): `Sanity.isEmpty_algHom_twoPoints` and `nontrivial_complementRing_twoPoints` prove no rational points and a nonzero localization. [`Examples/Degenerate`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/Degenerate.lean): `DegenerateExamples.poincarePolynomial_twoPoints` proves `1 + 2 X` for this same fixture. [`Examples/Ordinary`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/Ordinary.lean): `OrdinaryExamples.nonempty_basis_grading_twoPoints` gives a `Fin 2`-indexed degree-one basis for a supplied theory with characteristic-two coefficients. |
| 8. Projective hyperplane and points in P¹ | [`Examples/ProjectiveOrdinary`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/ProjectiveOrdinary.lean): `ProjectiveOrdinaryExamples.singleHyperplaneCohomologyEquiv` gives coefficient-algebra cohomology, and `singleHyperplanePolynomial` gives polynomial one. `poincarePolynomial_points` proves `1 + (m - 1) X` for every nonempty arrangement in P¹; `nonempty_basis_points_grading_one` and `nonempty_basis_points_projective_degree_one` give bases indexed by `Fin (m - 1)` over every coefficient ring. The three F₂-points fixture is checked explicitly: `exists_logMonomialBasis_decone_threePoints_zeroTheory` gives a decone word family with two length-one words, matching the integer polynomial. The proof constructs this family by empty/deletion recursion. Over zero coefficients, both basis and existential word-family statements allow other counts, so neither determines the Betti number. Its actual zero-theory diagonal is also checked. |
| 9. Zero coefficients | [`Examples/Degenerate`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/Degenerate.lean): `DegenerateExamples.different_basis_sizes_zeroTheory_twoPoints` gives bases with zero and two vectors for the very same group, together with the separate polynomial identity `bettiNumber 1 = 2`. The integer polynomial defines the Betti number; the empty/deletion recursion in the proof of `Sanity.exists_logMonomialBasis_zeroTheory_twoPoints` constructs a word family with matching counts. Over the zero ring, both bare basis and existential word-family conclusions allow other counts and do not determine this number. [`Examples/Ordinary`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/Ordinary.lean) and [`Examples/ProjectiveOrdinary`](../OrlikSolomon/Examples/ProjectiveOrdinary.lean) instantiate the affine and projective equivalences with actual zero theory. |
